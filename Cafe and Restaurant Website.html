Our Story & Passion

Crafting Warmth, Culture & Exceptional Flavor

At Buna & Bites Café, coffee isn’t just a beverage—it’s a timeless ritual of connection. Rooted in the heart of Addis Ababa, we blend the rich heritage of authentic Ethiopian Jebena Buna with a modern, relaxed dining experience.

Whether you are stopping by for your morning espresso fix, gathering with friends for a traditional coffee ceremony accompanied by aromatic frankincense, or enjoying our chef’s specialty burgers and fresh local bites, we welcome you with genuine Ethiopian hospitality (Tenayistillign!).
